FAQ

Questions, answered.

Native or cross-platform?
We advise per project. Native Kotlin for performance-heavy or hardware-heavy apps; cross-platform when speed-to-market and a shared codebase matter more.
Do you publish to the Play Store?
Yes — signing, store listing, review submission and staged rollouts are all handled.
Can it work without internet?
Yes. We build local-first with background sync so the app stays usable offline and reconciles when it reconnects.
Do you do iOS too?
Our focus is Android. For cross-platform builds an iOS target is often achievable — ask us during scoping.
